privacy policy

look, we're not rly interested in collecting user data. that's too much work.

we do however store your google account on our servers to make this thing tick. that's pretty much it.

what we collect

when you use rare numbers, we store:

  • your google account info (email, name, profile picture)
  • your collection of rare numbers (very important)
  • your credits balance (also important)

why we need this stuff

we need your google account to:

  • let you sign in without making yet another password
  • keep track of your rare numbers collection
  • remember how many credits you have
  • show your name on the leaderboard (if you're good enough)

how we use your info

we only use your info to:

  • let you play the game
  • that's it

we don't:

  • sell your data
  • share your info with third parties
  • send you marketing emails
  • track you around the internet
  • have the resources or interest to do any of that stuff

data security

we try to keep your data safe with standard security measures. but nothing's perfect.

your rights

you can:

  • ask us what data we have about you
  • ask us to delete your account
  • stop using the app whenever you want

if you want any of these things, email us at kev@thecollabagepatch.com

changes to this policy

if we change this privacy policy, we'll update it here. we might let you know in the app too, if we remember.

contact us

questions? concerns? rare number emergencies? email kev@thecollabagepatch.com
